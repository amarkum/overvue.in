Everything you own and owe, in one view.

No spreadsheets. No guessing. Overvue brings every rupee together so you always know where you stand.

Net worth, at a glance

A hero number for what you're really worth, broken down into liquid cash, money lent and debt — plus a trend chart over time.

Banks & cards together

Add savings and current accounts through one simple flow, and keep credit cards right next to them with payments tracked.

Budgets that keep up

Set one monthly limit and watch the bar fill as you spend, with a forecast of where the month will land, so overspending never sneaks up on you.

Stats that explain

Spending by category, trends against last month and your savings rate. Turn on Overvue AI if you'd like short notes on what changed.

Lent & borrowed

Log money you've lent to friends or borrowed from them. It counts toward your net worth, so nothing slips through.

Card bills, on time

Each card shows what you owe and when the bill is due, and logging a payment brings the balance down.

Subscriptions, before they renew

See what every repeat charge costs you a month and a year, what's due in the next 30 days, and get a reminder before each one.

Events to count down to

Trips, birthdays, the day a loan ends. Overvue counts down the days, and yearly events roll over on their own.

Every entry, day by day

Activity lists everything you've logged, grouped by day with the day's total, so any entry is easy to find and fix.

A closer look.

Real screens from the app, grouped by what they do. Flip the theme switch up top to see them in light or dark.

The small things, done well.

Quick to log, easy to read, and set up the way you like it.

Banks from your country

Choose your country and its banks and card issuers come first. Over 170 of them, across 27 countries.

Categories your way

Built-in categories cover the everyday. Add your own with an icon, and rename or remove them any time.

Logged in seconds

Type the amount and tap a category. The account, date and a note are one tap away when you need them.

Any month, any year

Look back at any month or year from Budget and Activity, with a quick month and year picker.

A gentle daily reminder

Pick a time and Overvue reminds you once a day to log what you spent. Off until you turn it on.

Overvue AI, if you want it

Ask about your money in plain words and get short, clear answers. It stays off until you switch it on.

Up and running in minutes.

Three steps from "where did it all go?" to a clear picture.

Add your accounts

Enter your banks and credit cards with their current balances. No bank passwords, ever.

Log as you go

Add an expense, income, card payment, or a loan to a friend with a couple of taps from the + button.

See the truth

Your dashboard updates instantly — net worth, budget progress and where your money went.

Spend with intention.

Set one monthly limit and let Overvue keep score. The bar turns from teal to amber to red as you spend, and a forecast shows where the month will land — so you notice early, not after it's gone.

  • One monthly spending limit, tracked daily
  • Left to spend and your per-day pace
  • Month-end forecast from your current spending
  • Dark mode by default — light and system themes in Settings

Your money. Your data.

Overvue is built so you stay in control of what it knows.

No bank logins

You enter the balances and transactions you choose. Overvue never asks for your bank passwords or card PINs.

Private by account

Your data syncs to your own account via Google Firebase, so it follows you across devices and isn't shared with others.

No ads, no selling

We don't show ads and we don't sell your personal data. Read the details in our Privacy Policy.
