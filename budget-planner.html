A monthly budget planner that warns you early

Set one monthly spending limit and Overvue keeps score. The bar turns from teal to amber to red as you spend, and a forecast shows where the month will land.

One simple monthly limit

No envelopes to juggle. Set a single monthly budget and Overvue tracks every expense against it, showing how much you've spent and the percentage used.

Left to spend, per day

See how much is left this month and what that means per day for the days remaining, so you know exactly what today's budget is.

Month-end spending forecast

Based on your current daily pace, Overvue projects your month-end spend and tells you whether you're on track to stay under budget, and by how much.

  • One monthly spending limit, tracked daily
  • Left to spend and your per-day pace
  • Month-end forecast from your current spending
  • Colour-coded progress from teal to amber to red

Frequently asked questions

How does the budget forecast work?

Overvue takes how much you've spent so far this month, works out your daily pace, and projects it to the end of the month.

Can I change my budget mid-month?

Yes. You can edit your monthly limit at any time and the progress and forecast update straight away.

More from Overvue

Net worth tracker

Track your net worth in one place. Overvue adds up your bank balances and money lent, subtracts card debt and loans, and shows the trend over time.

Expense tracker

Log expenses and income in a couple of taps, see spending by category, compare with last month and track your savings rate with Overvue.

Lend & borrow tracker

Keep track of money you've lent to friends or borrowed from them. Overvue counts it toward your net worth so nothing slips through.

Credit card tracker

Keep credit cards next to your bank accounts, track card payments and see when each bill is due with Overvue.
