Home / Features / Credit card tracker

Keep your credit cards and their due dates in view

Overvue keeps your credit cards right next to your bank accounts, tracks card spending and payments, and shows when each bill is due.

Cards and banks together

Add savings and current accounts and credit cards through one simple flow. Card balances count as debt in your net worth, so you see your true position.

Track card payments

Log card spending and bill payments as you go. When you pay a card from a bank account, both balances update.

Due dates at a glance

Each card shows when its bill is due, so a missed payment or late fee doesn't catch you out.

  • Credit cards alongside bank accounts
  • Card payments tracked
  • Bill due dates shown on each card
  • Card balances counted as debt

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to link my credit card?

No. You add the card and its balance yourself; Overvue never asks for card numbers, PINs or passwords.

Does Overvue remind me about card bills?

Each card shows its due date in the app, and you can turn on a daily reminder to keep your entries up to date.

More from Overvue

Net worth tracker

Track your net worth in one place. Overvue adds up your bank balances and money lent, subtracts card debt and loans, and shows the trend over time.

Expense tracker

Log expenses and income in a couple of taps, see spending by category, compare with last month and track your savings rate with Overvue.

Budget planner

Set one monthly budget, see what's left to spend per day and get a month-end forecast so overspending never sneaks up on you.

Lend & borrow tracker

Keep track of money you've lent to friends or borrowed from them. Overvue counts it toward your net worth so nothing slips through.

Subscription tracker

Track every subscription in one place: see your monthly and yearly cost, what renews in the next 30 days, and get a reminder before each charge.

Event countdown

Count down to trips, birthdays, loan end dates and tax deadlines in Overvue. Yearly events roll over on their own, so you never miss one.

AI money assistant

Overvue AI answers questions about your money and writes short notes on your month, from your own data. Off until you turn it on; chats aren't stored.
